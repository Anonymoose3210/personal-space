Games

I enjoy playing a variety of games, including...

Terraria

One of my favorite games that I've played ever since I was a kid. It's my first recommendation to anyone.

Celeste

Another of my favorite games that I played recently. It has both fun controls and a great story. Even though it's difficult, the checkpoints at the start of each screen make it manageable. Another great recommendation.

The Battle Cats

While this game isn't as popular as the other two, its strategic elements make it another of my favorite games and, in my opinion, one of the best mobile games.
